# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
B2B and government clients in Uzbekistan and the region: operators of solar plants, power grids, road networks, construction sites, farms and city administrations (hokimiyats). They land on the site to understand which monitoring direction fits their assets, then call or open a presentation.

## Product Purpose
UNB Group sells an intelligent infrastructure-monitoring platform: autonomous drones + AI defect detection + 2D/3D digital models + O&M task automation, in one closed loop. The site's success is a phone call (+998 94 388 88 82) from a qualified client; opening a direction's presentation is the secondary path.

## Positioning
One platform covering nine directions (mapping, solar, power lines, smart city, roads, construction, smart farming, drone delivery, hardware supply) instead of a single-vertical drone service.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site on GitHub Pages (CNAME unbgroup.uz); no framework, no bundler.
- `index.html` + `js/i18n.js` are the single source; `en/`, `uz/`, `zh/` are generated by `npm run build:pages`.
- Four languages: ru, en, uz, ch (public code `zh`). Every visible string lives in `js/i18n.js` under a `data-i18n` key.
- `deck.html` is the presentation viewer; its data (`js/decks-index.js`, `js/deck.js`, slide assets) were removed in the working tree on purpose (2026-09) while decks move to HTML.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: UNB Group. No raster logo currently; a text wordmark is used.
- Visual style pinned by the owner: "Dala" (dala.craftedbygc.com) — see DESIGN.md.
- Primary action: call. Only one filled (Electric Iris) button per view.

## Evidence on Hand
Stats already published on the site: 9 directions, 95%+ AI accuracy, −60% operating costs, ×10 inspection speed, 100% personnel safety. No testimonials, client logos or case studies exist — do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. The client's asset type is the entry point — directions must be scannable in seconds.
2. Every claim on the page is one the company already publishes.
3. Calling is always one tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on pure black; visible focus; keyboard navigation; reduced-motion respected. Cyrillic, Latin (Uzbek) and CJK text must all fit at 375px.
